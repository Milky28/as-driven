# Database license

The original selection and arrangement contributed to `data/` is licensed
under the [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Source citations are facts about where a claim came from; they do not imply
that third-party source material is relicensed here. Contributors must add
facts in their own words, preserve attribution, and comply with the source's
terms. Do not copy protected prose, images, tables, or large source extracts.

Before publishing a bulk import, a maintainer must review the source's reuse
terms. Importers create review candidates and never write directly to the
curated dataset for this reason.
